## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a fresh, encouraging, and distraction-free mobile learning environment. Designed for proactive learners and modern educators, the interface cultivates mental clarity, momentum, and steady achievement. 

Visual characteristics balance contemporary digital ergonomics with an uplifting academic aesthetic:
- **Card-Centric Modularity:** Content units (lessons, modules, progress indicators, flashcards) live inside soft, elevated surfaces that create an approachable visual rhythm.
- **Organic Freshness Meets Structural Utility:** Deep forest greens and luminous emeralds replace sterile corporate blues, invoking natural vitality and focus. Warm amber touches inject energy without provoking visual anxiety.
- **Friendly & Tactile:** Generously rounded corners, pill-shaped interactions, and fluid micro-surfaces soften the rigor of study tasks, making daily micro-learning habitual and delightful.

## Colors

The color palette reflects growth, clarity, and motivational milestones through calibrated light values.

### Roles & Semantic Mapping
- **Primary (`#0B6E4F`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors navigation headers, key calls-to-action, active progress trails, and primary brand accents. When pairing with gradient treatments, blend into `#14532D` at a 135-degree angle for visual weight on featured lesson cards.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` / `#059669`):** Bright Emerald. Used for completed milestones, streak badges, verified answers, and active toggles. Conveys forward velocity and success.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Energizing Amber. Reserved strictly for points, rewards, daily streaks, quiz timers, and attention prompts.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate/Navy. Primary text color providing crisp contrast without harsh pure black.
- **Muted Foreground (`#64748B`):** Cool Slate. Secondary captions, timestamps, instructor credentials, and placeholder values.
- **Canvas (`#F4FBF7`):** Soft Mint Wash. Provides an eye-soothing backdrop distinct from sterile stark white.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. Elevates modules, lesson sheets, and interactive elements from the mint canvas.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0` and `#D1FAE5`):** Ultra-subtle containment lines that maintain separation when cards rest adjacent to one another.

## Typography

Typography is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a balance of geometric discipline and warm, open apertures. Its humanist undertones maintain optimal readability across low-light reading environments and long course transcripts.

### Hierarchy & Mobile Scaling
- **Display & Headlines:** Used sparingly for daily greetings, module milestones, and congratulatory modal dialogs. Tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) maintains visual cohesion at bold weights.
- **Titles & Subtitles:** Structure card headings, active chapter lists, and quiz questions. The semi-bold (`600`) weight anchors vertical scans across busy dashboards.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (1.45 to 1.5) offer spacious room for multi-paragraph reading assignments, forum threads, and lesson summaries.
- **Labels & Badges:** Slightly tracked letter spacing (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) ensures miniature metrics, streak counters, and categorical tags remain immediately legible on 360px–414px mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a single-column, fluid mobile architecture designed to expand naturally into multi-column decks on tablet surfaces.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Base Grid:** Built on an 8-point spatial system (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`), with a 4-point micro-step (`0.25rem`) used strictly for metadata chips, inline icons, and input badges.
- **Screen Margins:** Mobile canvases maintain standard `1rem` (16px) gutters, widening to `1.5rem` (24px) on large phone formats and small tablets to frame content comfortably inside curved display corners.
- **Edge Containment & Breathing Room:** Cards and actionable containers utilize internal padding of `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px). Spacing between vertically stacked card modules is uniform at `12px` to `16px` to maintain scannable rhythm without excess height.
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance:** Screen content preserves a persistent bottom clearance of `5rem` (80px) to prevent floating tab navigation from occluding submit actions or sticky course progress bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp surfaces anchored by soft, ambient green-tinted glows and delicate structural borders.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** The `#F4FBF7` mint-white foundation. Non-elevated, flat backdrop.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Lists):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` or `#D1FAE5`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 110, 79, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Controls & Navigation):** Bottom navigation bars, primary sticky action bars, and search surfaces. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 110, 79, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Drawers & Popovers):** Quick-action lesson sheets, milestone modals, and quiz victory sheets. Shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 110, 79, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a 30% alpha slate backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a distinctively friendly, rounded shape language:
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 8px):** Checkboxes, nested progress segments, media thumbnail overlays, and small status indicators.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded-lg` / 16px to Pill / 9999px):** Primary buttons, filter chips, audio scrubbers, and search inputs adopt complete pill structures (`9999px`) to create clear touch affordability.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Course cards, lesson units, summary modals, and calendar day cells leverage generous 20px–24px radii, emphasizing an approachable, playful edtech presence.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), minimum touch target 48px height. Background is either solid `#0B6E4F` or a subtle 135° diagonal gradient from `#0B6E4F` to `#14532D`. Text is white, semi-bold (`label-lg`). Pressed state dims to 92% brightness with a 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary Action:** Mint-tinted pill surface (`#D1FAE5`) with deep emerald text (`#065F46`), no border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action:** Transparent pill with `#0B6E4F` label and standard horizontal padding.
- **Reward / Accent Action:** Solid `#F59E0B` background with white bold typography, designated for "Claim XP", "Streak Freeze", or "Upgrade to Pro".

### Inputs & Search
- **Pill Search Field:** Complete capsule shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), 48px height, filled with `#FFFFFF` and framed with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Prefix search icon in `#64748B`.
- **Form Inputs:** 52px height, 16px radius (`rounded-lg`). Off-white/slate surface (`#F8FAFC`) at rest, converting to `#FFFFFF` with a 2px `#10B981` border ring on focus. Helper and error text set at `body-sm`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips:** 36px height, pill-shaped. Unselected state features `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and `#64748B` text. Selected state switches to `#0B6E4F` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Streak & Level Badges:** Capsule shape, `#FEF3C7` (amber light) background with `#D97706` text and flame/star vector icon.

### Cards & Module Containers
- **Course Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 20px–24px corner radius, Tier 1 elevation. Includes full-width top visual thumbnail (12px top radius), progress track underneath, course title (`headline-sm`), instructor avatar chip, and bottom metadata row (duration, lessons count).
- **Interactive Quiz Card:** `#FFFFFF` card containing question prompt, accompanied by option selector cards below. Unselected answers feature `#F8FAFC` fill and `#E2E8F0` border; selected answers transition to `#ECFDF5` background with `#10B981` solid 2px outline and green checkmark.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 22px × 22px with 6px rounded corners (`rounded-md`). Rest state: 2px border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: solid `#10B981` fill with a white centered checkmark vector.
- **Radio Buttons:** 22px circular shape. Selected state displays an outer ring in `#0B6E4F` enclosing an 8px solid center dot.

### Lists & Progress Indicators
- **Lesson Item Row:** 64px minimum row height with internal vertical divider or connection line indicating learning journey path. Completed items display a circular check pill in `#10B981`; active items show an outlined pulsing dot in `#0B6E4F`; locked items display a subtle slate lock icon on `#F1F5F9`.
- **Linear Progress Bar:** 8px height, rounded full capsule track in `#E2E8F0` (or `#D1FAE5`). Filled bar utilizes the `#10B981` to `#059669` gradient with rounded ends.
