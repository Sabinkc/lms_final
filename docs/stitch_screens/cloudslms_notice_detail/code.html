Urgent Notice AY 2025–26 • Circular #48

Urgent: Unscheduled Campus Closure & Immediate Shift to Synchronous Online Classes

Posted by:
Aman Thakur (Principal Office)
Target Audience:
All Students, Parents & Faculty
Dispatched:
Oct 27, 2025 • 09:30 AM

Dear Students, Respected Parents, and Faculty Members,
Please be advised that in accordance with the latest advisory issued by the District Education Authority and adverse meteorological forecasts, the physical campus of Clouds Academy will remain closed for all standard academic and extra-curricular operations starting tomorrow, Tuesday, Oct 28, 2025 until further notice.

To guarantee complete academic continuity without interruption, all Grade 6 through Grade 12 scheduled sessions will transition smoothly into synchronous virtual classrooms hosted directly through the CloudsLMS portal. Students are instructed to join their respective subject sessions strictly per their existing standard timetable beginning at 08:30 AM sharp. Attendance will be monitored in real time via digital roll-call.

School transport services, physical lab examinations, and scheduled sports coaching clinics stand deferred. The administrative helpdesk and counseling helpline remain functional between 08:00 AM and 04:30 PM at +977-1-499200 or via helpdesk@cloudslms.edu.

Warm Regards,
Aman Thakur
Head of Administration & School Governance
